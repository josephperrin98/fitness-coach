# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

## What this application is

This is a personal training application built and operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not offered as a service to other people, has no other users, and is not intended for public registration. The only person whose data it processes is its owner.

## What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit authorisation through each provider's OAuth flow, this application reads:

**From WHOOP** — recovery scores, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep duration and stages, daily strain and physiological cycle data, workout strain and heart rate, basic profile information (name, email), and body measurements (height, weight, maximum heart rate).

**From Strava** — recorded activities and their associated metrics, including distance, duration, pace, heart rate and elevation.

No data is collected from any other source, and no data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

## Why it accesses it

Solely to generate, track and adapt the owner's own training plan, and to answer the owner's questions about their own training.

## Where data is stored

In a private database controlled by the owner. Access credentials and authorisation tokens are stored as environment configuration on the hosting provider and are never committed to this repository or included in its source code.

## Third parties

Training data is not sold, rented, published, or shared with any third party, with one exception the owner considers important to state plainly: to generate coaching output, relevant portions of this data may be sent to a large language model provider's API. That provider processes the data under its own terms in order to return a response. No other third-party transfer occurs.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as it is useful to the owner's training history and is deleted at the owner's discretion. Because this application has no users other than its owner, there is no deletion request process to operate.

## Revoking access

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any time from the WHOOP account settings or the Strava account settings, independently of this application. Revoking access there immediately stops any further data being read.
