    Progress

    This page comes once the route and drift exist (build steps 2 and 4). It will show:

    • the planned VDOT line, with a dot for each test you confirmed
    • weekly volume planned against done, a tick or cross and the adherence score for each week
    • pace adherence, a fitness curve, and your bests (1 km to half) for the last 4 weeks against the 4 before

    How the score works: falling short costs 1 point per 1% under target, going over costs 0.5 points per 1% over, a session done on another day of the same week loses 10 points, and a missed one scores 0. Within 5% of target is labelled done. A rest or a smaller session agreed at the morning check-in is scored against the new target. The numbers are computed by coach-core in Python; this page only draws them.
